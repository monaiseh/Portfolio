Lecturer in Computer Science & Programme Leader, MSc AI
AFG College — University of Aberdeen, Doha, Qatar
I am a researcher at the University of Aberdeen specialising in Responsible AI, Human-Centred AI, and Explainable AI. My work sits at the intersection of emerging AI techniques and user-centred design, with a focus on how people interact with, interpret, and trust AI-driven systems. I am a Fellow of the Higher Education Academy (FHEA).
